31 May – 4 June 2027 · Rogla, Slovenia

Once a year, PhD students working on evolutionary biology leave their labs and field sites to spend a week presenting their work, arguing about phylogenies late into the night, and hiking between talks. In 2027, we meet on the Pohorje plateau — organised by PhD students, for PhD students, since 1995.

Since 1995

An annual meeting, run by the students attending it

EMPSEB is organised independently each year by current PhD students in the host country, and supported by the European Society for Evolutionary Biology (ESEB). It moves to a different European setting every year, and has never had a fixed home.

The meeting brings together around 80–100 doctoral researchers working across every branch of evolutionary biology — from genomics to behaviour to macroevolution — to present their own research to an audience of peers, without the pressure of presenting to a room of senior faculty. Alongside the student talks and posters, 8–10 invited senior scientists give plenary lectures, run workshops, and share what they've learned about building a career in research.

Submitting an abstract

Pohorje, Slovenia

Venue & travel

Rogla

Rogla is a mountain plateau in the Pohorje range in eastern Slovenia, sitting at around 1,500 m among dense spruce forest. It's a quiet, self-contained setting — walking trails leave straight from the accommodation, and there's little to distract from the science (or the forest air).

Accommodation

All participants stay on-site for the duration of the meeting, within walking distance of the talk and poster rooms. Rooms are shared, in keeping with EMPSEB tradition.

Getting there

Nearest airports are Maribor (~45 min drive), Ljubljana (~1.5 hr), and Graz, Austria (~1.5 hr). A shuttle will run from Maribor and Ljubljana on the arrival day — details to follow closer to the meeting.

What to bring

Layers and sturdy shoes — late May in the mountains can mean sun, rain, or both in the same afternoon.
