31 May – 4 June 2027 · Rogla, Slovenia

Once a year, PhD students working on evolutionary biology leave their labs and field sites to spend a week presenting their work, arguing about phylogenies late into the night, and hiking between talks. In 2027, we meet on the Pohorje plateau — organised by PhD students, for PhD students, since 1995.

Since 1995

An annual meeting, run by the students attending it

EMPSEB is organised independently each year by current PhD students in the host country, and supported by the European Society for Evolutionary Biology (ESEB). It moves to a different European setting every year, and has never had a fixed home.

The meeting brings together around 80–100 doctoral researchers working across every branch of evolutionary biology — from genomics to behaviour to macroevolution — to present their own research to an audience of peers, without the pressure of presenting to a room of senior faculty. Alongside the student talks and posters, five keynote speakers give plenary lectures and share what they've learned about building a career in research.

Abstracts & registration

Submitting an abstract

Every participant gives a 10-minute talk, so please submit an abstract for it — on any topic in evolutionary biology, at any stage of completion. This is a room of fellow PhD students, so early or unfinished work is very welcome. On top of your talk, you can also present a poster if you like.

  • Abstract length: max. 250 words
  • Talk: 10 minutes, for everyone
  • Poster: optional, in addition to your talk
  • Deadline: to be announced

The registration fee covers accommodation on-site, all meals, the excursion day, and the conference dinner. Spaces are limited to around 90 participants, so early registration is recommended.

Alternative presentations

Alongside the regular talks and posters, there will be a segment for alternative presentations: a chance to share your research, or your life as a PhD student, in a completely different format. Sing a song, tell a story, run a game, draw a comic, or come up with something we haven't thought of. The only rule is to have fun with it.

Got an idea? Get in touch at empseb32@example.org and we'll find a spot for it.

Pohorje, Slovenia

Venue & travel

Rogla

Rogla is a mountain plateau in the Pohorje range in eastern Slovenia, sitting at around 1,500 m among dense spruce forest. It's a quiet, self-contained setting — walking trails leave straight from the accommodation, and there's little to distract from the science (or the forest air).

Accommodation

All participants stay at Hotel Rogla ***Superior, which is also where the meeting takes place, so the talk and poster rooms are just a short walk from your bed. The hotel has its own restaurant serving breakfast and dinner buffets with regional dishes, as well as a wellness area with a pool and saunas for unwinding after a long day of talks.

Rooms are shared between participants, in keeping with EMPSEB tradition. It's a great way to get to know your fellow PhD students. If you have any questions or special requirements regarding the shared rooms, please don't hesitate to contact us at empseb32@example.org.

Getting there

We encourage everyone to travel by train and bus wherever possible. Rogla itself has no rail connection, so the last stretch is by bus. The main stations to head for are:

Celje – the closest major railway hub, on the main Ljubljana–Zagreb line. From Celje bus station, buses run to Zreče, with a further local bus up to Rogla.
Ljubljana – the main international hub, with onward trains to Celje.
Maribor – the other big station nearby, with buses from Maribor bus station to Zreče.

From abroad, the usual route is an international train to Ljubljana, Maribor or Zagreb, then on to Celje. Timetables change, so please check connections before you book. We also want to organise a shuttle bus from Ljubljana to Rogla on the arrival day and back to Ljubljana on the departure day. We'll share the details closer to the meeting.
